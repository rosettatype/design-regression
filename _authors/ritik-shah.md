Ritik Shah is a wayfinding and information designer working at the intersection of typography, urban space, and multi-script legibility. He holds an MA in Communication Design (Information Design pathway) from the University of Reading, and his work looks at how multiple scripts and languages can coexist clearly within India’s urban environments. He previously worked with Applied Information Group in London, and now works with AtkinsRéalis as a wayfinding designer. He also serves as the World Regional Representative for India for the International Institute for Information Design.
